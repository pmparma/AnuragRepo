Signature Services

Everything a property needs, minus your presence.

From tenant onboarding to Khata paperwork, each service below is coordinated end to end and reported back with photos, notes and timelines — not handed off and forgotten.

Property & Estate Management

Complete administration, supervision, upkeep and owner reporting for your property, whether it's occupied, vacant, or somewhere in between. We coordinate everything on the ground — vendors, documentation, day-to-day oversight — and keep you informed with regular updates, so ownership stays hands-off without becoming hands-free of information.

Tenant Search & Onboarding

We handle the entire tenant lifecycle from the first listing to the final handover: sourcing genuine leads, conducting property visits, screening applicants for reliability, and managing the paperwork and move-in process. The goal isn't just filling a vacancy quickly — it's finding a tenant who'll actually take care of your property.

Rent Management

Rent tracking, timely reminders, and diligent follow-ups on any delays, backed by a clear monthly statement you can actually read. You'll always know what's been collected, what's pending, and what's been remitted — no spreadsheets to maintain yourself.

Inspections & Health Reports

Scheduled visits to your property with photo and video documentation, so you see the actual condition rather than take our word for it. Regular inspections catch small issues — a slow leak, a loose fitting — while they're still cheap and easy to fix.

Repairs & Maintenance

From a leaking tap to a full repainting job, we coordinate vendors, obtain estimates, supervise the work, and check it's actually done to standard before we call it complete. You approve the cost upfront; we manage everything after that.

Documentation Support

Rental agreements, identity verification, and secure digital record-keeping for everything related to your property. When you need a document six months from now, it should take one message, not a search through old emails.

Khata Transfer Assistance

Khata transfer involves real paperwork and real office visits. We handle the documentation checklist, the application, and the coordination with BBMP/GBA offices, so you're not the one standing in line.

e-Khata & Mutation Support

The digital Khata is increasingly the document that actually matters for transactions, loans, and approvals. We manage the application and follow-up on your behalf, including the mutation process where ownership records need updating.

Property Tax Assistance

Annual property tax has a rebate window if paid early, and real penalties if it's missed. We handle the calculation, documentation, and payment coordination so this is one deadline you never have to track yourself.

BESCOM Name Transfer

Electricity connections need to be formally transferred whenever ownership or tenancy changes. We handle the BESCOM application and coordination end to end, so the connection is correctly registered without you having to visit an office.

NRI Property Management

A complete remote management experience built specifically for owners living outside India — video inspection reports, a dedicated point of contact, and communication that works with your time zone, not against it.

Frequently Asked

Questions owners ask before signing up.

Don't see your question here? Message us directly — we're happy to walk through your specific situation.

Do I need to be in Bengaluru to sign up?

Not at all. Most of our NRI clients sign up entirely remotely — documents shared digitally, calls scheduled around your time zone.

What if I already have a tenant in place?

That's fine. We can take over an existing tenancy under Rental Management, picking up rent tracking and coordination from wherever things stand.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a few days of signing — an onboarding inspection is typically the first step, scheduled as soon as you're ready.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. As your situation changes — a property gets tenanted, or you want more hands-off support — we can move you to a different tier.

What happens if there's an emergency repair?

We're authorised to act immediately on anything affecting safety or habitability, and you're informed within 24 hours.

Do you only work with NRIs?

No — we manage properties for local owners too, including anyone simply too busy to self-manage a rental.

Is there a minimum contract length?

Our standard term is 12 months, renewing monthly after that — with a 30-day notice period either way.

Can you help if I just want one inspection done?

Yes — our one-time services cover exactly this, no ongoing commitment required.
